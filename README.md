# DNA of a Medical Startup

An interactive lecture on how a clinical problem becomes a business case, for medical students. About 60 minutes, 20 slides.

- [Ukrainian](https://mkorshe.github.io/medtech_dna/)
- [English](https://mkorshe.github.io/medtech_dna/en.html)

**Controls:** arrow keys or space to move, `O` slide overview, `F` full screen, `H` hide panels.

**Figures:** funding amounts are US benchmarks (Carta, PitchBook-NVCA 2026, Rock Health 2025) and are partly taken from secondary sources. Founder stakes and team and market numbers are illustrative.
